Papier Stories Co.

Every detail counts.

Papier Stories Co. is a Toronto wedding stationery and custom wedding signage studio creating bespoke pieces inspired by love stories, cultural traditions and meaningful moments.

We're not here to hand you a catalogue. Every piece — from a single place card to a full-scale installation — is designed around your celebration, from the first sketch to the final placement.

South Asian Celebrations

Designed to honour tradition, built around you.

South Asian weddings carry layers of tradition, symbolism and family that deserve more than an off-the-shelf sign. We work closely with couples to design signage and stationery that feels rooted in culture and personal to the two of you.

Multi-day celebrations

Cohesive signage across ceremonies, ensuring every event feels connected.

Language & script

Thoughtful incorporation of scripts, motifs and symbolism where it matters to your family.

Scale & spectacle

Large-scale installations designed to hold their own at grand, multi-generational celebrations.

The Studio

The little details make the biggest impact.

Papier Stories is run by Esther Daniel & Janeesh Mylvaganam, a husband-and-wife duo who bring a shared love of celebrating life's most meaningful moments to every piece they create.

One leads with creativity and visual storytelling; the other brings ideas to life through planning and production. Together, they design and produce every piece with intention, care and heart.
