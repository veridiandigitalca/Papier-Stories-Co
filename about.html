About Papier Stories

Turning memories into something you can hold.

Papier Stories was born from a love of celebrating life's most meaningful moments through thoughtful, beautifully crafted details.

Our Story

A studio built on paper, patience, and detail.

Papier Stories is about turning memories into tangible pieces people can see, hold and cherish.

We create custom-built wedding signage and stationery — designed to capture a couple's story through thoughtful paper and signage details. Every project starts the same way: by listening. What does your celebration mean to you, your family, and the people you're inviting into it? The design follows from there.

The little details make the biggest impact.
— Papier Stories Co.

The Founders

Esther Daniel & Janeesh Mylvaganam

Two people, one shared love of celebrating life's most meaningful moments.

Papier Stories is run by a passionate husband-and-wife duo. One focuses on creativity and visual storytelling; the other brings ideas to life through planning and production.

Together, they carefully craft every piece with intention, care and heart — from the very first sketch to the moment it's set up at your celebration.
