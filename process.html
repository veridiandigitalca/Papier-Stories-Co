How We Work

From first conversation to wedding day.

A collaborative process, built around your timeline and your vision — not a rigid package.

01

Share Your Vision

Tell us about your celebration, your aesthetic and what you're imagining — through our contact form or an initial consultation. There's no wrong way to describe it; a Pinterest board, a colour, or a feeling is a perfectly good place to start.

02

We Create

We develop a thoughtful design direction tailored to your event — considering your venue, your traditions, and the story you want your guests to feel as soon as they arrive.

03

Refine

We collaborate closely with you to refine the details — wording, materials, colour, scale — until every piece feels exactly right.

04

Bring It to Life

Your pieces are produced, checked over by hand, and prepared for delivery and setup — so all you have to do on the day is show up.
